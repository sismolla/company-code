Quote System & Supplier Connection

How does the quote system connect suppliers and customers?

Our quote system creates a direct supply chain connection:

  • Customer Requirements: Submit what you're looking for through our quote form
  • Supplier Matching: Your requirements are instantly visible to relevant suppliers
  • Direct Contact: Suppliers who can fulfill your needs will contact you directly

How do I submit my requirements to suppliers?

To submit your requirements:

  1. Click "Submit Your Requirements" button above
  2. Fill out the form with product details, quantities, and specifications
  3. Set your preferred contact method
  4. Submit - your request becomes visible to verified suppliers
  5. Suppliers with matching inventory will contact you directly

How do stock update notifications work on Pharmaገበያ?

Stock notifications help you stay updated when suppliers refresh their inventory:

  • If you follow a supplier, you’ll get notified when they add new products or update stock.
  • Notifications appear in your Pharmaገበያ account and can also be sent via email.
  • All new stock follows a first-come, first-served priority system.

Follow your preferred suppliers and enable notifications to never miss out on new stock opportunities.

Ordering Process

Why should I place my order through Pharmagebeya instead of direct contact?

Each product listing displays its current stock status:

  • First Come, First Served: Once you place an order in the system, the wholesaler's product is immediately marked as reserved under your name. This ensures that your order takes priority and must be fulfilled before any other requests made through direct contact or alternative channels.
  • Direct Contact: Orders placed through direct contact are not guaranteed. They remain subject to product availability and must receive explicit confirmation from the wholesaler before being considered valid.

How do I place an order with a wholesaler?

To place an order, follow these simple steps:

  1. Browse the wholesaler's product catalog.
  2. Select the products and click the product. You will see the "View Wholesaler" button that will lead you to the product wholesaler page where you can place your order.
  3. then click the add button to order.
  4. Review your order summary.
  5. Upload any required prescription documents.
  6. Submit your order request.
  7. then an email including your ordered product will be sent to you.

The wholesaler will review your order and contact you to confirm the details and arrange for payment.

Can I modify or cancel my order after submission?

Order modifications or cancellations must be requested directly with the wholesaler. Please contact them immediately if you need to make changes,. Once an order has been sent, it will be available for 7 consecutive days in the system. After this period, orders are no longer active.

The wholesaler has the authority to either confirm or cancel the order, and once this occurs, an immediate email will be sent to you at your provided email address.

Most Wholesalers can accommodate changes if requested before order processing begins.

How do I know if a product is in stock?

Each product listing displays its current stock status:

  • Stock Quantity: The available quantity in stock.
  • Out of Stock: The product is currently unavailable or has been reserved by a customer on a first-come, first-served basis.

For critical medications, we recommend contacting the wholesaler directly to confirm availability before placing your order.

Payments

What payment methods are accepted?

Payments are handled directly between the wholesaler and the customer. pharmagebeya does not process or manage any payment gateways. We strongly recommend using secure and common payment methods.

  • Avoid unconventional payment methods.
  • Always confirm payment details directly with the wholesaler before transferring funds.
  • We do not recommend making payments based solely on trust without verified contact information.

Is my payment information secure?

Since all payments are handled directly with the wholesaler, your payment security depends on the method and platform you use. We advise you to use established and reputable payment services and to verify the wholesaler's payment instructions carefully. pharmagebeya does not store or have access to any of your financial information.
