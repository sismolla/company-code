Who We Are

Pharma ገበያ is Ethiopia’s trusted B2B healthcare marketplace designed to connect suppliers and buyers in the pharmaceutical and medical device industries.

We provide a central digital platform where wholesalers can present their full product range—from essential medicines to advanced diagnostic machines—to healthcare facilities, pharmacies, NGOs, and bulk buyers.

Our goal is to make products discoverable, buyers connected, and transactions easier, ensuring no life is at risk due to lack of access.
